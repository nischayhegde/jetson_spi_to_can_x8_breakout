Applications
The 467 Series Fast-Acting SMF is an ultra small (0603 
size) thin-film device designed for secondary protection 
of circuits used in space constrained applications such 
as hand-held portable electronic devices. This series 
is 100% lead-free and meets the requirements of the 
RoHS directive. New Halogen-Free 467 Series fuses are 
available–to order use the “HF” suffix. See Part Numbering 
section for additional information..
• Cell phones
• Battery packs
• Digital cameras
• DVD players
• Hard disk drives.
