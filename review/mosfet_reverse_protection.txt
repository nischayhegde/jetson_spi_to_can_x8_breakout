V1 regulated 5 V input: P-channel reverse-hookup protection
Selected 2026-10-18; apply these connections in the schematic explicitly.

Parts (one each)
F2: Littelfuse 0466001.NR, 1 A fast-acting, 1206 (retained).
Q1: Alpha & Omega Semiconductor AO3401A, P-channel -30 V, SOT-23.
D2: Diodes Incorporated BZT52C6V8-13-F, 6.8 V Zener, SOD-123.
R_GATE: YAGEO RC0603FR-0710KL, 10 kohm +/-1%, 0.1 W, 0603.

Connection table
Input connector +5 V -> F2 -> Q1 pin 3 DRAIN.
Q1 pin 2 SOURCE -> +5V_PROTECTED -> CAN VCC pin 25 and LDO IN.
Q1 pin 1 GATE -> R_GATE -> common GND.
D2 pin 1 CATHODE / banded end -> Q1 pin 2 SOURCE.
D2 pin 2 ANODE -> Q1 pin 1 GATE.
10 uF input bulk capacitor -> +5V_PROTECTED and GND, AFTER Q1.
Connector ground -> common GND directly.
LDO output 3.3 V -> CAN VDD/VIO and logic supplies.
Keep nominal >=1 uF local LDO input and output capacitance.
Remove the previous PMEG3050EP rail-to-ground shunt diode.

Operation and sizing
Q1's intrinsic diode has its anode at drain, cathode at source. A correct
input starts the output through this diode, then the low gate turns Q1 on.
An initially unpowered output with reversed input leaves the intrinsic
diode blocking and Q1 off. Do not swap source and drain.

AO3401A gate absolute maximum is +/-12 V. Its maximum RDS(on) at VGS=-4.5 V
is 60 milliohm under the datasheet's 25 C conditions (85 milliohm at -2.5 V).
At 1 A: Vdrop ~= 60 mV; heat ~= 60 mW. At 0.75 A: 45 mV and 34 mW.
Resistance rises with temperature; verify the actual PCB temperature.
With the fuse's nominal 75 milliohm cold resistance, total cold drop at 1 A
is approximately 135 mV, before cables/PCB. CAN VCC must remain >=4.5 V.

The 6.8 V Zener normally stays below breakdown at a 5 V source-to-gate
voltage. Cathode at source and anode at gate clamp excessive negative VGS;
the gate resistor limits the resulting current. Its specified 6.4-7.2 V
range is at 5 mA/25 C; actual clamp voltage depends on current/temperature.
This is gate protection, NOT a 6.8 V supply clamp. Normal gate-resistor
current is essentially leakage, not 5 V / 10 kohm.

Limits to verify on the prototype
- Regulated 5 V input only: this is not 48 V miswire or positive-rail
  overvoltage protection, controlled inrush, or an electronic current limit.
- This single self-biased MOSFET does not implement ideal-diode reverse-
  current blocking: a charged output can feed a collapsing/shorted input.
  Rapid reversal with an already powered output needs separate evaluation.
- Keep the requested 1 A fuse, but check actual current/startup/temperature.
  Manufacturer continuous-current derating gives about 0.75 A at room
  temperature, 0.60 A in its 70 C example. The eight-channel 0.724 A load
  allowance before gates/pull-ups is not a measured average or guarantee.
- Use short, sufficiently wide power traces, and place Q1/Zener/resistor
  together. Review hot-plug, thermal behavior and minimum rail voltage.
