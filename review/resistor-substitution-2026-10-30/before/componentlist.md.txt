# SPI to eight-channel CAN PCB component list

Updated 2026-10-29 from the saved schematic and PCB. Quantities are for one board and count physical packages, including multi-unit logic ICs only once.

**21 purchase lines, 120 fitted components.** The PCB also contains 21 bare test pads and four mounting holes. These features require no purchased component. Package identities, references and purchasing fields are synchronized across the schematic and PCB. Circuit connectivity is unchanged.

**Standardized parts:** all eight 120-ohm CAN terminations use **RC1206FR-07120RL**, 1%, 0.25 W, 1206. J1 uses **M20-7832046** and the matching female socket footprint in the schematic, PCB and order list. [YAGEO resistor specification](https://yageogroup.com/component-documentation/download/specsheet/RC1206FR-07120RL); [Harwin socket specification](https://www.harwin.com/products/M20-7832046).
