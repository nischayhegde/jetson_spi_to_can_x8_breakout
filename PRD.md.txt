**CONTEXT**

- We are building a humanoid robot with 4 limbs and around 25 Robstride and Damiao actuators as well as some peripherals like tactile sensors, cameras, etc...

- Main embedded computer: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super

- Our team is the embedded design team. We are tasked with designing and building all the embedded communications between the computer and the motors/peripherals.

- The NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super only has a singular CAN channel. It has two SPI channels with 2 chipselects each.

**GOAL**

- Build an 2xSPI-8xCAN expander board.

- Write the Linux drivers for the expander.

**REQUIREMENTS**

- Cheap options for parts.

- All parts must be in stock on mouser or digikey and have associated symbol and footprint libraries for KICAD

- No unnecessary complexity. For example, there is no point in choosing a separate CAN transceiver and controller if it is not immensely cheaper/highquality than a component which combines a CAN transceiver and controller. Another example: no point in choosing a powered oscillator when we could use a passive oscillator.

- Good power management/regulation, overcurrent protection, signal integrity, decoupling.

- Relatively small form-factor.

- Galvanic isolation between digital signals and power system.

- 5V max 2A in.

**THINGS TO KEEP IN MIND**

- Bitrates per controller will have to be around 1Mbit/s to interface with the Robstride and Damiao motors. NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super is not the bottleneck at 1.7 GHz. Oscillator should be at 40Mhz.

- This means parasitic capacitance will slow the slew rate and parasitic inductance will affect signal integrity.

- Chipselect output and interrupt input might be pretty high frequency so we need the driver to bypass the kernel and set/read the GPIO memory address directly.


**GPIO BANK CHIP-SELECT REQUIREMENT (2026-10-17)**

- Each physical SPI bus retains its two native active-low Jetson chip selects and uses one independent GPIO bank selector to address four CAN controllers.
- Implement the selection with AND and NOT gates on the 3.3 V host side, before the galvanic isolators. Both native chip selects high must deselect all four controllers.
- Linux must own and serialize the bank GPIO with SPI transactions. Evaluate the standard spi-mux/gpio-mux framework with the exact JetPack kernel; do not toggle the bank independently from userspace. This supersedes treating direct GPIO register access as mandatory above.
- Use separate bank GPIOs for the two SPI buses. Change bank only with both native CS lines inactive; budget logic/isolation delay, CS setup/hold and previous-device MISO release.
