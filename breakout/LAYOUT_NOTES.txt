Compact SPI-to-eight-channel CAN FD layout

Board size: 81 x 80 mm (18.6% less area than the original 99.5 x 80 mm outline).
The mounting-hole change extended the left edge 4 mm from the previous
77 x 80 mm layout without moving existing components or tracks. The later
standardization changed only R26's package, rotation and local CAN tracks.
All 141 existing footprints are retained, plus four board-only mounting holes.

Corner mounting holes H1-H4: 3.2 mm non-plated drills for M3 screws/standoffs.
Each center is 4 mm from the two adjacent board edges; spacing is 73 x 72 mm.
From the top-left board corner, centers are (4,4), (77,4), (4,76), (77,76) mm.
Absolute KiCad centers: (60,51.5), (133,51.5), (60,123.5), (133,123.5) mm.
Allow hardware up to 7 mm outside diameter on either face. Each hole has a
7 mm copper keepout on all four copper layers and 7.5 mm front/back courtyards.
The keepout excludes the drill itself; normal NPTH copper clearance protects
that opening. Mounting footprints and their keepouts are locked together,
marked board-only, and excluded from BOM and component-position exports.
The project-local MountingHole_3.2mm_M3_7mm_Keepout footprint stores these rules.

J1 grounds 6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34 and 39 connect to GND, following the
Jetson Orin Nano developer-carrier 40-pin header pinout. The seven previously
unused ground pads were connected and the existing planes refilled.
Connector positions and orientations have changed; selected passives and test
pads are on the back side. The Jetson socket is between two four-channel banks.

Four copper layers:
  F.Cu: local IC, crystal, bypass and signal routing.
  In1.Cu: continuous ground plane; no signal tracks.
  In2.Cu: 3.3 V pour with selected SPI and control routes.
  B.Cu: signal routing and selected small components.

All 24 MCP251863 bypass connections are 2.425 mm from IC pin pad to cap pad.
Oscillator signals stay on F.Cu without vias.
Approximate maximum header-to-controller paths, including series-resistor span:
  SPI0: clock 75.4 mm, MOSI 58.8 mm, MISO 47.7 mm.
  SPI1: clock 67.5 mm, MOSI 95.4 mm, MISO 48.1 mm.

Standardized on 2026-10-29:
  All eight 120-ohm CAN terminations use RC1206FR-07120RL, 1%, 0.25 W, 1206.
  R26 remains on B.Cu at its original center, rotated 180 degrees. Only its
  two local CAN routes to J10 were replaced to fit the larger pads.
  All other component centers, sides, pad geometry, tracks and vias are intact.
  J1's symbol, value and MPN identify the M20-7832046 female socket already
  represented by its footprint. TP13/TP14 identify M20-9990246 male headers.
  The 28 bypass capacitors use C1 and C21-C47, value 100n, separate MPN fields.
  All 120 purchased packages have matching schematic/PCB MPN/Manufacturer.
  The schematic sheet is A1; one grid-aligned translation brings its existing
  contents onto the sheet. Pin-to-net connectivity is exactly unchanged.
  Model paths resolve to the project-local 3dmodels directory.

Checked with KiCad 10.0.7 after standardization: zero ERC findings, zero DRC
violations, zero unrouted items, zero schematic/PCB parity issues. All eight
channel checks, 16 bank-select states and 256 interrupt states pass. Project
rules and exclusions are unchanged. The four mounting holes are preserved.

These static checks do not establish SPI timing margin, unpowered-input
safety, LDO/fuse thermal margin, oscillator/EMC performance or mechanical fit.
Existing electrical-review qualifications remain applicable; see
../review/standardization-2026-10-29/standardization-report.txt.

Collaboration: use a separate Git checkout for each person, pull before editing,
and coordinate ownership of the PCB file. KiCad lock files only describe an
open editor instance in that working folder; they are not a Git collaboration
or remote-presence mechanism. Lock files, local preferences and recovery
backups are intentionally excluded from version control.
