Compact SPI-to-eight-channel CAN FD layout

Board size: 77 x 80 mm (22.6% less area than the original 99.5 x 80 mm outline).
All 141 component footprints are retained.
J1 grounds 6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34 and 39 connect to GND, following the
Jetson Orin Nano developer-carrier 40-pin header pinout. The seven previously
unused ground pads were connected and the existing planes refilled.
Connector positions and orientations have changed; selected passives and test
pads are on the back side. The Jetson socket is between two four-channel banks.

Four copper layers:
  F.Cu: local IC, crystal, bypass and signal routing.
  In1.Cu: continuous ground plane; no signal tracks.
  In2.Cu: 3.3 V pour with selected SPI and control routes.
  B.Cu: signal routing and selected small components.

All 24 MCP251863 bypass connections are 2.425 mm from IC pin pad to cap pad.
Oscillator signals stay on F.Cu without vias.
Approximate maximum header-to-controller paths, including series-resistor span:
  SPI0: clock 75.4 mm, MOSI 58.8 mm, MISO 47.7 mm.
  SPI1: clock 67.5 mm, MOSI 95.4 mm, MISO 48.1 mm.

Checked with KiCad 10.0.7: zero DRC violations, zero unrouted connections,
and zero schematic/PCB parity issues. No DRC exclusions were added.
Mechanical fit, signal integrity and hardware operation require validation.

Collaboration: use a separate Git checkout for each person, pull before editing,
and coordinate ownership of the PCB file. KiCad lock files only describe an
open editor instance in that working folder; they are not a Git collaboration
or remote-presence mechanism. Lock files, local preferences and recovery
backups are intentionally excluded from version control.
